## Skills Validated

- Application Design and Build (Containers, Pods, Deployments)
- Application Deployment (Rolling Updates, Canaries)
- Application Observability (Liveness/Readiness probes, logging, monitoring)
- Pod Design (Resource limits, env variables, secrets)
- Services and Networking (Ingress, NetworkPolicies)

## Preparation & Experience

To obtain the CKAD, I mastered Kubernetes primitives, declarative configuration, and container orchestrations. The exam is 100% hands-on, requiring rapid cluster configuration under pressure.
