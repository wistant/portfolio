## Skills Validated

- SDLC automation (CI/CD pipelines)
- Configuration management and Infrastructure as Code (IaC)
- Monitoring, logging, and incident response
- High availability, fault tolerance, and disaster recovery

## Preparation & Experience

This certification validates the ability to automate testing, deployment, and operations on AWS. Key areas of focus included AWS CodePipeline, CloudFormation, Systems Manager, and implementing advanced deployment strategies like blue/green and canary releases.
