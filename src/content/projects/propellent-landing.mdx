## Technical Highlights & Implementation

Propellent is built on a modern, robust tech stack, ensuring high performance, scalability, and a premium user experience.

- **Next.js & TypeScript**: Provides a solid, type-safe foundation for a performant and maintainable application, leveraging server-side rendering for optimal SEO and speed.
- **Tailwind CSS**: Utilized for rapid and consistent UI development, ensuring a clean, modern, and responsive design across all devices.
- **Recharts**: Integrated for powerful and customizable data visualization, enabling clear and actionable insights from complex analytics.
- **SaaS & Analytics Focus**: Engineered specifically for SaaS applications, with a strong emphasis on analytics to drive business intelligence and growth strategies.
